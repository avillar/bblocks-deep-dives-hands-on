# OGC Building Blocks Deep Dive

Exercise register for the OGC Building Blocks deep-dive video series: a water body survey
observation with a custom result and a provenance trace.


Built step by step in the Hands-on videos of the series. Each video has a `<slug>-task` tag
(starting point) and a `<slug>-solved` tag (end state).


## Building Blocks

### `ogc.deepdive.waterBodySurveyResult` — Water Body Survey Result

**Type:** schema

The result of a water body survey: surface elevation, depth and area.
